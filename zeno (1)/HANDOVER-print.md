<h2 id="1-project-overview">1. Project overview</h2>
<h3 id="11-what-is-zeno">1.1 What is zeno?</h3>
<p><strong>zeno (زينو)</strong> is a <strong>local (proximity-based) job marketplace</strong> for Saudi Arabia, focused on <strong>blue-collar, hourly, part-time, seasonal, and full-time service jobs</strong> — hospitality, retail/cashier, delivery &amp; logistics, cleaning, events, maintenance, security, crafts. It connects <strong>job seekers</strong> with <strong>nearby employers</strong> and lets them <strong>communicate directly (in-app or via WhatsApp)</strong>.</p>
<p>The product spans three surfaces:</p>
<ul><li>A <strong>mobile app</strong> (Arabic, RTL) for job seekers and employers.</li><li>A <strong>public marketing website</strong> (Arabic, RTL) for discovery, employer conversion, and content.</li><li>An <strong>employer web dashboard</strong> for posting and managing jobs and applicants.</li></ul>
<h3 id="12-what-problem-does-it-solve">1.2 What problem does it solve?</h3>
<ul><li><strong>For job seekers:</strong> finding legitimate nearby work is slow and fragmented. zeno surfaces the closest opportunities first (map + distance), lets them apply in one tap, and connects them to the employer immediately.</li><li><strong>For employers:</strong> reaching nearby, ready-to-work candidates is hard. zeno lets a business post a job in minutes and receive/manage applicants from a single place.</li><li><strong>Trust:</strong> verified businesses, clear job terms, no fees charged to seekers.</li></ul>
<h3 id="13-who-are-the-users-three-roles-confirmed-as-selectable-roles-in-the-prototype">1.3 Who are the users? (three roles — [CONFIRMED] as selectable roles in the prototype)</h3>
<table><thead><tr><th>Role</th><th>Arabic</th><th>Primary surface</th><th>Purpose</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><strong>Candidate / job seeker</strong></td><td>المرشّح / الباحث عن عمل</td><td>Mobile app</td><td>Browse, search, apply, chat</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Employer</strong></td><td>صاحب العمل</td><td>Mobile app <strong>and</strong> web dashboard</td><td>Post jobs, review applicants, chat</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Admin</strong></td><td>الإدارة</td><td>Mobile-app admin view (prototype)</td><td>Oversight: stats, jobs, reports</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p>Registration also supports an <strong>account type</strong> distinction at signup: <strong>individual (فرد)</strong> vs <strong>organization (منشأة)</strong> — [CONFIRMED] as a toggle; its downstream effect is [INFERRED].</p>
<h3 id="14-how-the-platform-works-happy-path">1.4 How the platform works (happy path)</h3>
<ol><li>User installs app → sees onboarding → picks role.</li><li>Signs up with <strong>Saudi mobile number</strong> → verifies via <strong>4-digit OTP</strong>.</li><li>Completes a <strong>registration/profile</strong> step.</li><li><strong>Candidate:</strong> browses/searches jobs, views on map by distance, opens a job, applies in one tap, tracks application status, chats with employer (in-app or WhatsApp).</li><li><strong>Employer:</strong> posts a job, receives applicants, accepts/rejects, chats, can pause/activate the listing.</li><li><strong>Admin:</strong> monitors platform stats, jobs, and reports.</li></ol>
<h3 id="15-main-modules">1.5 Main modules</h3>
<ul><li><strong>Auth &amp; Onboarding</strong> (splash, onboarding carousel, phone, OTP, registration, account-type)</li><li><strong>Job discovery</strong> (browse, category filter, text search, advanced filters, nearby/map, job detail)</li><li><strong>Applications</strong> (apply, my-applications with status tracking)</li><li><strong>Messaging</strong> (in-app chat + WhatsApp deep-link handoff)</li><li><strong>Profile</strong> (candidate profile, edit profile)</li><li><strong>Employer tools</strong> (dashboard/overview, post job, manage listings + pause/activate, applicants + accept/reject, employer chat, employer account)</li><li><strong>Notifications</strong></li><li><strong>Admin</strong> (overview, jobs oversight, reports)</li><li><strong>Public web</strong> (landing, jobs browse+detail, pricing, about, company profile, contact, terms)</li></ul>

<h2 id="14-business-logic-rules">14. Business logic &amp; rules</h2>
<blockquote><p>Rules marked [CONFIRMED] are enforced in the prototype. Others are [INFERRED] and need PM sign-off.</p></blockquote>
<ol><li><strong>Proximity ranking [CONFIRMED]:</strong> Nearby shows <code>distance ≤ radius</code>, sorted ascending. Production must validate a real code.</li><li><strong>Notifications [CONFIRMED types]:</strong> acceptance, new message, job match, application update. <strong>[INFERRED]:</strong> delivery (push/SMS/in-app), triggers, preferences.</li><li><strong>Failure scenarios [INFERRED, undesigned]:</strong> network loss, OTP failures, geolocation denial, job closed mid-apply, concurrent accept when vacancies exhausted, duplicate phone numbers, employer editing a job with active applicants.</li></ol>

<ul><li><strong>User</strong> (id, phone, role[candidate|employer|admin], accountType[individual|organization], createdAt, status).</li><li><strong>CandidateProfile</strong> (userId, name, age, nationality, city, jobTitle, yearsExperience, skills[], brief, profileUrl).</li><li><strong>EmployerProfile / Organization</strong> (userId, companyName, verified[bool], about, cover, location).</li><li><strong>Job</strong> (id, employerId, title, category, companyName, district/location{lat,lng}, type, salary, unit, gender, nationality, vacanciesCount, distance(derived), postedAt, contactMethod, description, status[active|stopped|closed], views).</li><li><strong>Application</strong> (id, jobId, candidateId, requestNumber, status[submitted|review|accepted|rejected], channel, createdAt).</li><li><strong>Conversation</strong> (id, jobId?, participants[], lastMessage, lastTime).</li><li><strong>Message</strong> (id, conversationId, from, text, time, deliveryStatus).</li><li><strong>Notification</strong> (id, userId, type, title, body, time, unread).</li><li><strong>Report</strong> (id, targetType, targetId, reason, status) — admin.</li><li><strong>Category</strong> (id, label, icon) — 10 fixed seed values (see §4.6 / cats list).</li></ul>
<h3 id="162-relationships">16.2 Relationships</h3>
<ul><li>User 1–1 Candidate/Employer profile (by role). Employer 1–N Jobs. Job 1–N Applications. Candidate 1–N Applications (≤1 per Job). Job/Application ↔ Conversation ↔ Messages. User 1–N Notifications.</li></ul>
<h3 id="163-auth-authz">16.3 Auth &amp; authz</h3>
<ul><li>Phone + OTP authentication; session/JWT. Role-based access (candidate/employer/admin). Ownership checks (employer only manages own jobs/applications). <strong>Admin gating undefined [§20].</strong></li></ul>
<h3 id="164-cross-cutting">16.4 Cross-cutting</h3>
<ul><li><strong>Search &amp; filters:</strong> server-side query by text, category, type, gender, nationality; pagination (lists are unbounded in prototype — add pagination/infinite scroll).</li><li><strong>Geo:</strong> geolocation capture, job geocoding, distance calc + radius query, map tiles/provider.</li><li><strong>Uploads [INFERRED]:</strong> avatars/logos/attachments are not in the prototype but likely (profile images, CVs, company logos) — confirm.</li><li><strong>Notifications:</strong> push (mobile), possibly SMS; triggers on status change / new message / job match.</li><li><strong>WhatsApp:</strong> deep-link composition; phone privacy/masking policy.</li><li><strong>Pagination/sorting:</strong> sort options shown on web jobs (nearest/newest/highest salary) require server support.</li></ul>

<blockquote><p>Requirements that <strong>cannot be determined from the UI alone</strong>. Nothing here is invented functionality — each item is either implied-but-unspecified or entirely absent. <h3 id="a-authentication-account-lifecycle">A. Authentication &amp; account lifecycle</h3>
<ol><li><strong>OTP rules</strong> — *Why:* prototype accepts any/<code>4829</code>; length is 4 but expiry, attempt limits, resend cooldown, lockout are absent. *Ask:* code length/expiry? max attempts? resend cooldown? SMS provider? OTP autofill?</li><li><strong>Phone validation &amp; uniqueness</strong> — *Why:* only digit-stripping/≤10 is enforced. *Ask:* exact Saudi formats accepted? one account per number? number change flow?</li><li><strong>Account recovery / change number / delete account / logout-everywhere</strong> — *Why:* no such screens exist (only simple logout). *Ask:* is recovery needed (OTP-only may suffice)? account deletion (Terms mentions "request deletion" but no UI)? data retention?</li><li><strong>Session &amp; token policy</strong> — *Why:* no session UI. *Ask:* JWT lifetime, refresh, multi-device, forced logout.</li></ol>
<h3 id="b-roles-permissions-account-type">B. Roles, permissions &amp; account type</h3>
<ol><li><strong>Account type effect</strong> — *Why:* فرد/منشأة is a toggle with no visible consequence. *Ask:* can individuals post jobs, or is posting employer-only? Different verification/limits?</li><li><strong>Admin access &amp; permissions</strong> — *Why:* admin screens are read-only mock tabs with no auth gate or actions. *Ask:* who is admin? how do they log in (same OTP? separate console?)? what actions (approve verification, take down jobs, resolve reports, manage users)? permission granularity? audit logging?</li></ol>
<h3 id="c-employer-verification-trust">C. Employer verification &amp; trust</h3>
<ol><li><strong>Verification workflow</strong> — *Why:* verified badge is shown but no submission/approval UI exists. *Ask:* what documents/criteria? who approves (admin)? does unverified block posting? SLA?</li></ol>
<h3 id="d-jobs-applications">D. Jobs &amp; applications</h3>
<ol><li><strong>Application status transitions</strong> — *Why:* labels exist; triggers for <code>review</code> and timing are unspecified. *Ask:* what moves submitted→review? auto or manual? SLAs? can employer reopen a rejected app? expiry?</li><li><strong>Vacancies &amp; closing logic</strong> — *Why:* <code>count</code> exists but nothing decrements or closes a job. *Ask:* does filling all vacancies auto-close? can candidates apply to a full/paused job? closed-job state design?</li><li><strong>Paused-job visibility</strong> — *Why:* pause flips a badge but prototype still lists the job. *Ask:* should paused jobs be hidden from discovery/search/map immediately?</li><li><strong>Job editing constraints</strong> — *Why:* edit reuses the create form. *Ask:* can core fields change after applicants exist? notify applicants of changes?</li><li><strong>Duplicate/spam &amp; content moderation</strong> — *Why:* no validation on job content. *Ask:* moderation rules, banned content, reporting-to-action flow (Reports tab has no actions).</li></ol>
<h3 id="e-discovery-geo-search">E. Discovery, geo &amp; search</h3>
<ol><li><strong>Distance/geolocation source</strong> — *Why:* <code>distance</code> is hardcoded mock data. *Ask:* map provider? geocoding? live user location vs chosen city? offline/denied-permission behavior? distance units/rounding?</li><li><strong>Search relevance &amp; pagination</strong> — *Why:* client-side "contains" match, unbounded lists. *Ask:* server search semantics (fuzzy/synonyms/Arabic normalization)? pagination/infinite scroll? sort definitions (nearest/newest/highest salary)?</li></ol>
<h3 id="f-messaging-whatsapp">F. Messaging &amp; WhatsApp</h3>
<ol><li><strong>Messaging backend</strong> — *Why:* messages are local, no delivery/read states or history load. *Ask:* real-time transport (websocket/push)? read receipts? attachments? moderation? blocking?</li><li><strong>WhatsApp handoff details</strong> — *Why:* pre-filled template + <code>app.zeno.sa/profile/&lt;req&gt;</code> link are mock. *Ask:* is the profile URL public? phone privacy/masking? consent? which number is used?</li></ol>
<h3 id="g-notifications">G. Notifications</h3>
<ol><li><strong>Notification delivery &amp; preferences</strong> — *Why:* only an in-app list with 4 seed items; no settings screen. *Ask:* channels (push/SMS/in-app)? exact triggers? per-type opt-in? read/clear actions?</li></ol>
<h3 id="h-payments-monetization">H. Payments / monetization</h3>
<ol><li><strong>Pricing &amp; billing</strong> — *Why:* Pricing page tiers/prices are placeholder marketing; no checkout, no subscription state, no "upgrade" flow (sidebar "ترقية الحساب" is a dead CTA). *Ask:* real tiers/prices? payment provider? what's gated by tier (posting limits, priority, verification)? invoicing/VAT?</li></ol>
<h3 id="i-uploads-media">I. Uploads &amp; media</h3>
<ol><li><strong>File uploads</strong> — *Why:* no upload UI exists, but profiles/companies/CVs likely need images/docs. *Ask:* are avatars/logos/CV/attachments required? formats/size limits? storage/CDN?</li></ol>
<h3 id="j-sharing-content">J. Sharing &amp; content</h3>
<ol><li><strong>Share a job</strong> — *Why:* no share affordance anywhere. *Ask:* is job sharing (deep link, social) in scope?</li><li><strong>Company profiles &amp; content data</strong> — *Why:* company page and marketing stats (12k+, 4.9, etc.) are placeholder. *Ask:* real data sources? who edits company profiles? CMS for marketing/blog?</li></ol>
<h3 id="k-localization-legal-compliance">K. Localization, legal, compliance</h3>
<ol><li><strong>RTL/Arabic scope</strong> — *Why:* scope says Arabic-only; no i18n framework. *Ask:* will English ever be needed? number/date/currency (SAR) formatting, Hijri dates?</li><li><strong>Legal content</strong> — *Why:* Terms/Privacy text is drafted design copy. *Ask:* must be replaced/approved by legal; PDPL (Saudi data protection) compliance, consent, data residency?</li></ol>
<h3 id="l-states-resilience-responsive">L. States, resilience &amp; responsive</h3>
<ol><li><strong>Loading/error/offline states</strong> — *Why:* largely undesigned (see §12). *Ask:* standard skeletons, error/retry patterns, offline behavior, timeouts.</li><li><strong>Responsive/mobile-web layouts</strong> — *Why:* web surfaces are designed at fixed desktop widths (see §19). *Ask:* target breakpoints? mobile-web dashboard/jobs layouts? tablet behavior? Is dashboard expected on mobile web or app-only?</li><li><strong>Analytics/metrics definitions</strong> — *Why:* overview/admin show views, acceptance rate, "+18%", 48h avg hire as mock numbers. *Ask:* exact metric definitions, time windows, and event tracking needed to compute them.</li></ol>
